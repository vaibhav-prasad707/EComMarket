## Bain & Company — How India Shops Online 2025
Source: https://www.bain.com/insights/how-india-shops-online-2025/
Accessed: 2026-08-18

- India’s e-retail market is approximately $60B GMV and has the world’s second-largest online shopper base.
- E-retail growth in 2024 was 10%–12%, below the historical rate of more than 20%.
- Bain projects $170B–$190B of e-retail GMV by 2030 and more than 18% growth over the next six years.
- Three in five new shoppers since 2020 came from Tier-3 cities or smaller; 60% of new sellers since 2021 came from Tier-2 or smaller cities.
- In 2024, more than two-thirds of e-grocery orders and one-tenth of e-retail spend happened on quick-commerce platforms.
- Quick commerce is forecast to grow more than 40% annually to 2030; the competitive set includes Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Flipkart Minutes, Myntra M-now, BigBasket BB Now, and Amazon Tez.
- Hyper-value commerce reached 12%–15% of Indian e-retail GMV; value platforms have traction with lower-middle-income consumers in Tier-2 and smaller cities.

Use: market framing and cross-platform insight, not as a substitute for platform-level figures.
