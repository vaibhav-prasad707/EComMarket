# Design Direction: Signal & Shelf

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Signal & Shelf
Very brief intro: A newsroom-meets-operator dashboard that treats marketplace data as a living market map: editorial, confident, and legible at a glance.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Bazaar Atlas
Very brief intro: A warm cartographic research interface using regional color blocks and route-like visual connectors to make platform coverage feel expansive and geographic.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Quiet Ledger
Very brief intro: A restrained, print-inspired research notebook with cream paper, ink typography, and sparse analytical marks for a more academic tone.
Probability: 0.08

## Chosen approach: Signal & Shelf

### Design Movement
Editorial data journalism with the compositional discipline of a financial terminal and the humanity of a magazine spread.

### Core Principles
1. Make the evidence feel inspectable: every figure carries a source cue, as-of date, or disclosure label.
2. Use asymmetry to create hierarchy: a narrative left rail, analytical center, and compact utility actions rather than a centered dashboard grid.
3. Contrast dense research tables with generous editorial whitespace so the page remains approachable.
4. Treat the horizontal-versus-vertical split as a visual system, not merely a filter.

### Color Philosophy
Use ink navy for trust and source discipline, warm paper for approachability, and a single ownable saffron-orange for active insights and calls to action. A muted teal marks vertical/specialty operators, while a restrained coral marks horizontal scale. The palette should feel like a well-edited business journal, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

### Layout Paradigm
A fixed editorial masthead and a staggered, two-column research canvas. The left column anchors the thesis and filter controls; the right column carries the market snapshot, charts, and table. On mobile, the rail becomes a compact disclosure strip above the analysis.

### Signature Elements
1. A split-color “market ribbon” that visually compares horizontal and vertical platform counts.
2. Source chips that look like tiny footnotes and reveal evidence context on hover/focus.
3. Orange “signal lines” that connect insight statements to the corresponding chart or metric.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like inspecting a research notebook: filters are immediate, sort controls are explicit, chart points reveal context, and any unknown value stays visibly unknown rather than becoming a false precision. The UI should reward curiosity without hiding the methodology.

### Animation
Use short 160–220ms transitions for filter states, chip emphasis, table row hover, and chart crosshair appearance. Use a single staged entrance for the masthead, snapshot, and first chart; do not animate every table row. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Display: Fraunces, 600–700, for the title and insight headlines. Body/UI: Source Sans 3, 400–600, for labels, tables, filters, and notes. Use uppercase letter-spaced micro-labels sparingly for metadata. Numeric highlights use Source Sans 3 with tabular numerals.

### Brand Essence
A source-first map of India’s e-commerce landscape for analysts and operators who want breadth without hand-waving. Personality: forensic, generous, grounded.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound decisive but not sensational. CTAs are specific and useful. Microcopy names uncertainty plainly.

Example lines:
- “Scale is visible. Disclosure is not.”
- “Trace the category mix before you call it a market.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact “S/S” mark built from two offset shelf bars: one coral for horizontal marketplaces and one teal for specialty verticals. The bars align like a data table header, with a small saffron notch indicating a source signal.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Saffron — #E9943A.

## Style Decisions
- Use a warm paper base, ink navy type, coral horizontal accents, teal vertical accents, and Signal Saffron for active analytical emphasis.
- Prefer editorial asymmetry over a generic centered dashboard.
- Never use a fabricated metric, review, rating, testimonial, or filled-in value where a source does not disclose it.
